Shipping Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Where we deliver

We deliver across India. If you'd like an order sent outside India, message us on WhatsApp and we'll let you know if it's possible and what it would cost.

Made to order

Every piece is crocheted by hand after you order. When we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we'll tell you when it will be ready to ship. Custom pieces and larger orders may take longer.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges depend on your location and the size of your order. We'll confirm the exact amount on WhatsApp before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Dispatch and tracking

  • We pack every piece carefully so it arrives in perfect shape.
  • Once your order is shipped, we'll share the courier and tracking details with you on WhatsApp.
  • Delivery times depend on your city and the courier, and can be longer during festivals or bad weather.

Your address

Please check that your name, phone number, pincode and address are correct when you place your order. If a parcel is returned because of an incorrect address or because nobody was available to receive it, re-shipping charges may apply.

Questions

For anything about your delivery, message us on WhatsApp, call +91 72049 89869 or email info@crosell.in.
