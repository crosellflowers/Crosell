How to order

Ordering is as easy as sending a message.

No accounts and no card details on this website. Pick your blooms, tell us where to deliver, and send your order to us on WhatsApp. We'll take it from there.

Start shopping Ask us a question
  1. 01

    Pick your blooms

    Browse the collection and tap Add to bag on anything you love. Open a piece to see more photos and choose how many you'd like.

    Browse the collection →
  2. 02

    Place your order

    Open your bag and tap Place your order. Fill in your full name, phone number and delivery address, and add a note if you'd like a gift message or different colors.

Good to know

The small print, made simple

Payment

Nothing is charged on this website. Once your order is confirmed, we'll share payment options in the chat.

Delivery

Enter your pincode and address when you place your order. We'll confirm delivery options and cost before you pay.

Timing

Every piece is crocheted by hand after you order. We'll give you a clear timeline when we confirm.

Custom colors

Want a different color mix? Mention it in the order note, or message us about a custom piece.
